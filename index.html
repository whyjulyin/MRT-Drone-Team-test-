01 / MISSION BRIEF

WHY BUILD THIS DRONE?

The rover can encounter terrain obstructions and disrupted line-of-sight communications. Our quadcopter is designed to act as an elevated relay and aerial observer.

Mission objectives

01

Airborne RF relay

Bridge the base station and rover across terrain obstructions while hovering at a proposed 60 m AGL.

02

Aerial reconnaissance

Scout driving routes, locate mission equipment and support elevated ArUco marker identification.

03

Endurance under constraints

Balance flight time with the 5 kg mass cap and a mandatory dummy payload equal to battery mass.

Key engineering challenges

01 / CABLE LOSSES

Operating from an enclosed base station

A proposed 20 m LMR400 antenna extension introduces estimated RF losses of 4.36 dB at 2.4 GHz or 2.56 dB at 900 MHz, before connector effects.

02 / DATASHEET UNCERTAINTY

Designing before physical validation

Motor performance, battery mass and electronics efficiency are initially based on manufacturer data; conservative margins and staged testing reduce uncertainty.
